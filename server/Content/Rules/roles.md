# D&D Rules: Roles

There are two real-world roles to choose from when playing Dungeons & Dragons: Dungeon Master and 
Player.

## Dungeon Master

The Dungeon Master (DM) builds a story containing a series of locations and events and guides the
players through the adventure. They also enforce and decide the game's rules.

## Player

The Player controls a ficitional character of their own design within a group of other Players.
They explore locations and events created by the DM, choosing to respond to situations in
any way they can imagine (within the rules).
