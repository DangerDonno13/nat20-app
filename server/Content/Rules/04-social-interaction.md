# D&D Rules: Social Interaction

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

The Dungeon Master plays the nonplayer characters (NPCs) your party meets.

## Making a Request

Say what your character wants and how they ask. You can speak in character or describe
your approach. A guard might let you pass if you show a permit but hesitate if you ask
to skip an inspection.

## Influence

A willing NPC agrees, and an unwilling NPC refuses. If the NPC is hesitant, the DM
calls for an ability check that fits your approach, such as Charisma (Persuasion) for a
sincere appeal. The default DC is the higher of 15 and the NPC's Intelligence score.
You have Advantage on a check to influence a Friendly NPC and Disadvantage with a
Hostile NPC.
