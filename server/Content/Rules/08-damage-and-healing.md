# D&D Rules: Damage and Healing

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

## Hit Points

Damage reduces your current Hit Points (HP). Healing restores HP up to your maximum.
Temporary HP absorb damage first. If you gain a new pool of Temporary HP, choose
which pool to keep.

For a given damage type, Resistance halves damage (round down), Vulnerability doubles
it, and Immunity prevents it.

## At 0 HP

A character who drops to 0 HP usually falls Unconscious. Damage kills outright if,
after dropping a character to 0 HP, the damage left over equals or exceeds their
HP maximum.

If you're at 0 HP and aren't Stable, roll a Death Saving Throw at the start of
your turn. A d20 result of 10 or higher succeeds. Three successes stabilize you,
and three failures kill you. A natural 1 counts as two failures. A natural 20
restores 1 HP.

An ally can take the Help action and pass a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check to
stabilize you. Taking damage at 0 HP normally adds a failed save, or two on a
Critical Hit.
