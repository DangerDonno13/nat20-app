# D&D Rules: Combat

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

A round represents about six seconds in the game. Each combatant takes a turn each round.

## Initiative

When combat starts, combatants normally make a Dexterity check for Initiative. Take turns
from the highest total to the lowest and repeat that order each round. A surprised
combatant rolls Initiative with Disadvantage.

## Your Turn

You can move up to your Speed and take one action. You can split your movement around the
action. Use Attack to strike, Dash to gain extra movement equal to your Speed, or
Disengage to avoid provoking Opportunity Attacks with your movement that turn. You can
take one Bonus Action if a feature or rule gives you one.

## Reactions

A Reaction lets you respond to a trigger, even on another creature's turn. Once used,
it's available again at the start of your next turn. For example, a creature you can
see using its movement to leave your reach can provoke a melee Opportunity Attack
just before it leaves.
