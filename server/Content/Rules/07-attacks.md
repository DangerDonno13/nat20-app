# D&D Rules: Attacks

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

## Hitting a Target

Choose a target within your reach or range. Roll a d20 and add the relevant ability
modifier and your Proficiency Bonus if you're proficient with the weapon. Most melee
weapon attacks use Strength, and most ranged weapon attacks use Dexterity. A Finesse
weapon lets you choose either. The attack hits if the total meets or beats the target's
Armor Class (AC).

## Damage and Critical Hits

On a typical weapon hit, roll the damage dice and add the same ability modifier used
for the attack roll. A natural 1 on the die misses. A natural 20 on the die is a
Critical Hit. Roll the attack's damage dice twice and apply the usual modifiers once.

## Cover

Cover protects the target from effects coming from the other side of the obstacle.

| Cover | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Half | +2 to AC and Dexterity saves |
| Three-Quarters | +5 to AC and Dexterity saves |
| Total | Blocks direct targeting |
