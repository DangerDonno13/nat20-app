# D&D Rules: Exploration

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

## Travel

Tell the Dungeon Master where the party is headed and give a marching order. That order
helps determine who spots danger or triggers a trap. When the DM tracks travel pace, a
Fast, Normal, or Slow pace affects how far you travel and how easily you notice danger
or move quietly.

## Searching

Describe where you look and what you're trying to find. The DM might call for Wisdom
(Perception) to spot a hidden tripwire or Intelligence (Investigation) to work out how
the trap operates.

## Light and Vision

Dim Light gives Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight. Darkness
normally blocks sight. Within its range, Darkvision lets you see in Darkness as if it
were Dim Light.
