# D&D Rules: Playing the Game

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

An adventure moves between exploration, social interaction, and combat.

## The Play Loop

The Dungeon Master (DM) describes the situation. Players say what their characters try
to do. The DM resolves their actions and describes what happens. Opening an unlocked door
can happen immediately. Picking a lock might call for a roll.

If a spell, feature, or other specific rule gives an exception to a general rule, follow
the specific rule.

## Reading Dice

The number after the `d` is the number of sides on each die. For `2d6 + 3`, roll two
six-sided dice, add their results, then add 3. A `d20` is a twenty-sided die.
