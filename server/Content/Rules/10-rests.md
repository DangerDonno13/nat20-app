# D&D Rules: Rests

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

You need at least 1 Hit Point (HP) to start either kind of rest.

## Short Rest

Spend an hour resting. Afterward, you can spend Hit Dice to heal. For each die you
spend, roll it and add your Constitution modifier. Regain that many HP, with a
minimum of 1 HP per die. Decide whether to spend another die after each roll.

## Long Rest

A Long Rest normally takes at least eight hours, including at least six hours of
sleep and up to two hours of light activity. It restores lost HP and all spent Hit
Dice and removes one level of Exhaustion. Wait at least 16 hours after finishing
before starting another Long Rest.

Some class features and spell slots also recharge after a rest, as their rules specify.
