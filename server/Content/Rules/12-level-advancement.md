# D&D Rules: Level Advancement

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

## Gaining a Level

If your group tracks Experience Points (XP), you level up when your total reaches
the next threshold on the advancement table. The Dungeon Master can also award
levels when the party reaches story goals or plays a set number of sessions.

## Updating Your Character

Add one Hit Die. Increase your Hit Point maximum by your Constitution modifier
plus either the result of rolling that die or your class's fixed amount, with a
minimum increase of 1. Record your new class features and any changes to spells.
Your Proficiency Bonus increases by 1 at character levels 5, 9, 13, and 17.
