# D&D Rules: Creating a Character

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

Use a character sheet to record your choices and the numbers you'll use during play.

## Class and Origin

Your class sets your starting features and training. Your background grants an Origin
feat and helps determine your proficiencies and ability scores. Your species gives you
traits such as your Speed. Choose starting equipment from your class and background.

## Ability Scores

Assign scores to Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
One method uses the standard array of 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, and 8. Of the three abilities
listed in your background, raise one by 2 and another by 1, or raise all three by 1.
For each modifier, subtract 10 from the score, divide by 2, and round down.

## Starting Numbers

Use your class to find your starting Hit Points and Hit Die. Your Proficiency Bonus is
+2 at level 1. Without armor or a shield, your Armor Class is usually
`10 + Dexterity modifier`.
