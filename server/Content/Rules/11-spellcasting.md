# D&D Rules: Spellcasting

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

## Spells and Slots

Your class or feature tells you which spells you can prepare. Cantrips are level 0
spells and require no slot. For spells of level 1 or higher, you normally spend a
spell slot of the spell's level or higher. Across all your spellcasting on a turn,
you can spend only one spell slot.

## Casting a Spell

Check the spell's casting time, range, duration, and components. Verbal (V) means words,
Somatic (S) means gestures, and Material (M) means specified materials. For a spell
attack, add your spellcasting ability modifier and Proficiency Bonus to the d20.
If a spell calls for a save, its DC is `8 + those two modifiers`.

## Concentration

You can maintain one Concentration effect at a time. When you take damage, make a
Constitution save to keep it. Use DC 10 or half the damage taken (round down),
whichever is higher, up to DC 30. Becoming Incapacitated ends Concentration.
