# D&D Rules: Conditions

*2024 rules (5.5e)*

A condition changes what a creature can do. The condition or the effect that caused
it tells you when it ends.

## Common Conditions

- **Prone.** Your attack rolls have Disadvantage. Attack rolls against you have
  Advantage from within 5 feet and Disadvantage from farther away. Standing costs
  movement equal to half your Speed.
- **Poisoned.** Your attack rolls and ability checks have Disadvantage.
- **Restrained.** Your Speed is 0 and can't increase. Your attack rolls and Dexterity
  saves have Disadvantage, and attack rolls against you have Advantage.

## Repeated Effects

Multiple instances of a condition have separate durations and usually share one set
of effects. Exhaustion accumulates. Each level subtracts 2 from D20 Tests and 5 feet
from your Speed. At level 6, you die.
